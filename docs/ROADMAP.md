# Roadmap

Plans, not promises; the order may change.

## Next

- **Code signing** for the installer, so Windows SmartScreen does not warn.
- **3D spaces**:
  - focal length from a rectangle of known shape (a door, a sheet of
    paper);
  - floor snapping for moving several layers at once and for the 3D
    workspace's G.
- **Masks**:
  - painted masks that change over time (keyed);
  - a better filter for weak lines when finding lines for a 3D space.

## Later

- Partial erasing of pen strokes.
- Recording dial moves (such as volume) live while playing.
- Palm rejection options for touch screens.
- A check of Korean input methods while editing text on the canvas.

## Known issues

- Free views in the 3D workspace show only the space's layers and the
  camera.
- Text in a 3D space is edited in the panel, not on the canvas.
- Very large recorded pen layers can lag slightly while playing.
- The app icon's pressure stroke is soft at 16 px.
- Pen and touch input are tested on one tablet setup (Super Display with
  an S Pen), not automatically.

Ideas and bug reports are welcome in
[issues](https://github.com/seven-ash/DriftingVOID/issues).
